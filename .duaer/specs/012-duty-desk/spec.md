# 012 Duty desk and reports (v1.0, part 1)

## Problem

On-call staff work alert by alert with no shared queue, no response deadlines,
no record of what one shift hands to the next, and no periodic summary for
managers. Acknowledgement and closure times are not stored, so response and
resolution times cannot be measured.

## Decisions (confirmed with the user, 2026-11-06)

- SLA defaults: response high 15 min / medium 2 h / low 24 h; resolution
  high 4 h / medium 3 days / low none. Editable by admins.
- Reports: daily at 9:00, weekly at 9:00 on Mondays (server time zone), each
  push channel opts in to daily and/or weekly. Default: no report push.
- Handover is ad hoc (no fixed shifts): it covers the time since the previous
  handover (12 hours for the first one).
- Out of scope: MLPS 2.0 (等保) mapping and evidence export.

## Change

- Store `acked_at` on alerts (human acknowledgement only) and `acked_at` /
  `resolved_at` on incidents (first claim; closure, cleared on reopen).
- Duty desk page (admin landing page): work queue of open incidents and open
  alerts outside incidents, with SLA countdown, claim and acknowledge;
  metrics for today / 7 / 30 days (mean time to respond, resolve, detect,
  SLA compliance, false positive rate); handover with preview, notes, history
  and push to all enabled channels; SLA settings.
- Mean time to detect = attack simulation detection latency (real attack
  start times are unknown).
- Reports page: daily and weekly reports generated by the leader instance,
  stored once per period (unique constraint), pushed only within 6 hours of
  the scheduled time; view and Markdown export.

## Acceptance

- Queue ordering, claim / acknowledge, SLA due times, metrics and handover
  covered by `internal/server/duty_test.go` on SQLite and PostgreSQL.
- Report schedule, per-channel delivery, no duplicates, no stale push covered
  by `TestReports` / `TestReportPeriod`.
- Existing databases upgrade in place (new columns and tables).
- `check:i18n`, `check:guide`, `check:refs`, `build`, `go test ./...` pass.
- Verified in a browser against a running server.
